Contract P — font decision surface

Choose the exact face the Garden will be drawn against. Under Contract P the typeface is chosen before the art, because proportional drawing is made against specific glyph advances.

Everything below renders through the real candidate font binaries, embedded via @font-face from ./fonts/. No sample is shown through a system font. That is deliberate: the defect this whole exercise exists to correct was art reviewed in one font and painted in another.

1. Provenance — the exact resources

All three are SIL Open Font License 1.1 and therefore redistributable. Locally installed proportional faces (Georgia, Palatino, Optima, Baskerville, Hoefler Text) are absent from this list because they are system fonts that cannot legally be bundled into the product.

3. Diagnostic — what proportional rendering does to the accepted art

Correction. An earlier version of this panel said the candidates “shear this art equally” because of proportional advances. That was the wrong mechanism. Measured against each font's cmap: Literata 0/12, Source Serif 4 0/12, EB Garamond 2/12 of the twelve non-ASCII characters this art uses. The browser is substituting a fallback font for nearly every glyph — which is why all three columns below look almost identical. They are largely not showing the candidate faces at all.

What this panel actually demonstrates is per-glyph fallback: the original defect, reproduced under new fonts. It is still the evidence for why Contract P requires the ten fixtures to be redrawn rather than re-reviewed, and it still cannot tell the candidates apart — for a more decisive reason than first stated.

4. Still outstanding after this page

Approving a face here settles step 4's face question. It does not settle the size: 15px is what the worksheet and the atlas declare and what the accepted art was signed off at, 13px is what the product currently paints, and the right answer depends on art that does not exist yet. Line height, weight, style and letter spacing become part of the runtime contract in step 6. Scene captures of the current-root and frozen-legacy viewers are not included here — they show monospace box art and would say nothing about a proportional face choice.
